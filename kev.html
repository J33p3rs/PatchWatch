What the number means

Patch Watch counts entries in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue by each record's dateAdded. The source is CISA's official cisagov/kev-data GitHub mirror of the canonical catalogue.

This measures when CISA added a CVE to KEV. It does not mean exploitation began that month, and it is not a vendor patch count or a count of newly published CVEs.

The vendor/project comparison uses normalised rolling trends rather than raw totals. It asks whether a tracked disclosure/assignment series is moving faster or slower than the rate at which vulnerabilities are being added to KEV.
